Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Brightmade Studios LLC ("Brightmade," "we," "us") makes apps. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our apps, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • This website has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Each app collects only what it needs to work, and each one is listed below.
  • You can ask us to see or delete your information at any time.

This website

This site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. Like any web host, Google receives basic technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it briefly in server logs under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't use this information to identify you.

The site does not set cookies or use analytics, and the fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

When you email us

If you write to contact@brightmadestudios.com, we keep your email address and message so we can reply and follow up. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Our apps

Each app has its own section here that lists exactly what it collects, why, and which service providers process it. The same details appear in the app's privacy label on the App Store and its Data safety section on Google Play.

No apps have been published yet. Each app's section will be added here before that app launches.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run our website and apps, such as Google (Firebase) for hosting and app data. They process information only on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments. Each app's section names the providers it uses.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires. When you delete your account, we delete its data as described on our account deletion page.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email contact@brightmadestudios.com from the address linked to your account, and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children

Our apps and this website are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and to collect as little as possible.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let app users know in the app.
