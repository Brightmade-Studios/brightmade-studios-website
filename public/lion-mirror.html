Lion Mirror

Move, and the lion moves with you. A friendly 3D lion copies every move. Wave, jump and dance, then raise both hands to snap a photo.

Book this station $100 an hour

What happens

Step up

Kids stand in front of the screen and the lion wakes up.

Move

Wave, jump, spin. The lion copies everything, live.

Hands up

Raise both hands and the camera counts down for a photo.

Scan and save

A QR code appears so the family keeps the picture.

Great for

  • Big rooms with lots of kids
  • Kids who would rather move than pose
  • Parents who want a photo of the fun, not just a smile

The details

  • One screen. We bring: laptop, tv and webcam.
  • $100 an hour, or $250 an hour with both other stations (the Full Experience).
  • Every photo comes with a scan-to-save QR code.

Photos and privacy

  • Kids never need an account, an email address or a phone number to join in.
  • Photos are taken on our equipment. Each one gets its own private link, and only its QR code opens it.
  • The Elfie Selfie Station and Letter to Santa use AI. For those, the photo is sent to an AI service from Google (Gemini) so it can make the elf portrait or read the letter. The Lion Mirror follows movement right on the device and does not send photos to AI.
  • After your event we send the host a copy of the photos and delete them from our system. Any family can skip a station or ask us to delete their photo.

Questions about photos or privacy? Write to us before the day and we will answer plainly.
