Questly Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Questly is a chores and rewards app for families, made by Brightmade Studios LLC ("Brightmade," "we," "us"). Children use it, so this policy is also our notice to parents under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It covers the Questly app we set up for each family, and the Get Questly and referral forms on this website. Our general Privacy Policy covers the rest of this website.

The short version

  • Each family's Questly is its own separate app with its own database. No other family can see it.
  • Children do not give Questly their email, phone number, address, location, voice or photo. They have no way to message anyone.
  • Questly has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and nothing to buy inside it. We never sell information or use it for advertising.
  • A parent gives us written consent before we create a child's sign in.
  • A parent can see everything Questly holds about their child, and can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who is responsible

Brightmade Studios LLC operates Questly and is responsible for the information in it.

How each family's Questly is set up

When a family buys Questly, we create a separate project for them on Google Firebase, which we manage. That project holds the family's sign ins and database, and serves their copy of the app at its own web address. The app is installed to the family's devices from that address. Families do not share a database with each other or with any other Brightmade product.

What we collect from parents

Before we set up your Questly

  • Your contact details, from the Get Questly form or by email: your name, email address and phone number, whether someone referred you and their name if you give it, and anything you choose to tell us.
  • What we need to build your copy: the first names of the people in your family, what your children call you, the chores and rewards you want, and photos or descriptions of each person so we can draw their character.
  • Your consent: the signed consent form described under "Parental consent."
  • Billing records: what you bought, what you paid and when.

In the app

  • Sign in: we create each parent's login with an internal address that is not a real email inbox, and a password. Google Firebase Authentication stores the password in a form we cannot read.
  • What you set up and answer: chores and their standards, rewards and their prices, photos you add to rewards, settings such as bedtime and saving goals, and every answer you give to a request (approve, not yet with a reason, or no).
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications on your phone, a notification token for that phone, so we can send it alerts. Only parents' devices receive notifications.

What we collect from children

Children use Questly only through a sign in a parent asked us to create, after the parent has given consent. Through it, Questly collects:

  • Their sign in: an internal login, not a real email address, with a password the parents keep. The child's first name, which the parent gives us.
  • What they do in the app: when they mark a chore done, the rewards they put in a basket or ask for, their saving goal, and their star balance.
  • Drawings: pictures they draw in the app to show what they would like. Drawings are pictures made with the app's pens, not photos or uploads.
  • Daily feeling faces: which of three faces they picked for their day, if they pick one.
  • Technical information: like any website, Google's servers receive the device's IP address and browser details to deliver the app, and the app keeps a sign in token on the device so the child stays signed in.

Children cannot type messages to anyone, share anything outside their family's Questly, or make anything public. Questly does not collect a child's email address, phone number, home address, precise location, contacts, voice, or any photo or video of them.

The characters

Each person in your family appears in Questly as a cartoon character. To make them, we use the photos or descriptions you send us. We create the characters with Google's Gemini image generation service, under terms where Google does not use what we send it to train its models, and then finish them by hand. We do not create face templates or any other biometric identifier.

We delete the photos you sent within 30 days after you approve the finished characters, and do the same for any photos you send for your character update. The finished characters become part of your family's app. Like the app's other pictures, they are files on your app's web address, which is not listed or linked anywhere, and each file is named with the person's first name.

How we use information

  • To build, run and support your family's Questly, including your character update.
  • To send parents notifications that a child is waiting on an answer.
  • To keep Questly secure and working, and to fix problems.
  • To answer your questions, send quotes and invoices, and apply a referral discount.

We do not use anyone's information for advertising, profiling or marketing, we do not sell or rent it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence. Sign in IDs and notification tokens are used only to run Questly: to keep people signed in, deliver notifications, and keep the service secure.

Notifications

When a child asks for something, Questly sends a notification to each parent phone that has notifications on. It names the child and counts what is waiting, for example "Ava has 2 waiting." It never says what the request is and never lets anyone answer from the notification. The notification is shown on the parent's lock screen according to that phone's settings.

Who we share it with

We disclose information only to the service providers that run Questly for us, each of which is bound by its own data processing terms and security commitments, and only for the purpose listed:

  • Google (Firebase): sign in, the database, hosting the app, and delivering notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Google (Gemini): making the characters, from the photos or descriptions you send us.
  • Cloudflare: runs the small service that checks a child's request, counts what is waiting and sends the notification. It reads the family's database for that purpose and does not keep a copy of it.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services: carry the notification text to a parent's device, depending on the device and browser.
  • Resend: delivers the Get Questly and referral form to our inbox by email.

Information is stored and processed in the United States. We do not disclose children's information to anyone else, except if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of Brightmade, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

Parental consent

Before we create a child's sign in, we send the parent a consent form that explains what Questly collects from children and how it is used. A parent or legal guardian signs and returns it. Until we have it, we do not create the child's sign in and Questly collects nothing from that child. If we learn that we have collected information from a child without consent, we delete it.

Parents' rights

A parent can, at any time:

  • Review what Questly holds about their child. Almost all of it is visible on the parents' side of the app, under History and Drawings. We will also send you a copy of everything we hold if you ask.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete their child's information.
  • Stop any further collection, by withdrawing consent. We then turn off that child's sign in.

Inside the app, entries cannot be deleted by anyone, so that both parents and children can trust the record. Deletion is done by us: email contact@brightmadestudios.com from the email address we have on file for your family. We may ask you to confirm you are the child's parent before we act, and we will act within 30 days. Parents can ask for the same things about their own information.

How long we keep information

  • Your family's Questly (sign ins, chores, rewards, requests, answers, star history, drawings and settings): for as long as your family uses Questly, so the family's record stays complete. When you ask us to close your Questly, or after a refund, we delete the whole project within 30 days.
  • Daily feeling faces: deleted automatically after 14 days.
  • Photos sent for characters: deleted within 30 days after you approve the characters.
  • Notification tokens: removed when the phone stops accepting notifications, or with your Questly.
  • Form submissions, emails and consent forms: for as long as we support your Questly and up to three years after, so we can show what was agreed and consented to. If you contact us and do not buy, we delete your details within one year.
  • Billing records: for as long as tax law requires.

We never keep children's information indefinitely, and we keep it only for the reasons above.

Security

Every request to a family's database is checked against access rules on Google's servers, so a person can only read and write what their role allows, and a child's tap can only ever create a request. All connections are encrypted. Only the Brightmade people who build and support Questly can reach a family's project. We keep a written information security program for children's information, with a named person responsible for it, and review it at least once a year. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your family, we will tell you promptly.

Your other rights

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights over your information under local law, and we will honor them. To use any right in this policy, email contact@brightmadestudios.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we collect, use or share children's information, we will tell parents by email first, and ask for new consent where the law requires it.
