The Hunt

A private surprise-trip app that turns the announcement of a journey into an experience.

Rather than simply telling someone about a trip, The Hunt gives them a countdown, a series of surprises that unlock as the days and miles pass, and a reveal designed to be remembered. It was built once, for one person, around one trip.

Weeks before the trip

Anticipation without spoilers

The recipient receives a private app and nothing more. There is no itinerary and there are no hints, only a countdown and occasional short notes that build curiosity without giving anything away. One reads: "It's not a dinner. It's not a party. Stop guessing."

The reveal

A reveal worth waiting for

The experience builds to its central moment: wrapping paper tears away on screen to reveal the surprise. The paper-tear animation is custom made, and nothing is disclosed in advance, so the recipient sees the surprise for the first time at exactly this point.

On the road

Capsules that unlock along the journey

Sealed capsules unlock as the trip unfolds, each containing a note, a photo or a riddle to be read aloud. Capsules can open at a scheduled time, upon arrival at a specific location, or on demand with a tap. A notification is delivered the moment a capsule opens, even when the app is closed and the phone is locked.

On the road

Every capsule, a small surprise

Each opened capsule is added to a growing pile in the corner of the screen, a visual record of the surprises discovered along the way.

Then a trip companion

An itinerary that responds

Once the trip is underway, the app becomes a travel companion. It presents a day-by-day plan, lets guests react to each stop with a tap, and gives them a simple way to suggest changes without a group conversation. If a guest would prefer to skip an early morning stop, they say so and the plan is updated.

Run from a phone

One control panel, managed live

The entire experience is managed from a single control panel on a phone. Capsules can be released early, messages can be sent at any moment, and the plan can be monitored in real time as suggestions arrive.

The recipient never sees the control panel. To them, the trip simply seems to stay one step ahead, which for those few days it does.

  • Countdown and teaser notes
  • Capsules that open by time, place or tap
  • Instant notifications, even with the app closed
  • Paper-tear reveal
  • Live trip plan with suggestions
  • Phone control panel
