The Hunt

A private surprise-trip app that turns "we're going somewhere" into an experience, not just an announcement.

Instead of telling someone about a trip, The Hunt hands them a countdown, a string of little surprises that unlock as the days (and miles) go by, and a reveal moment they will actually remember. Built once, for one person, around one trip.

Weeks before the trip

Curious, not spoiled

The person it is for gets a private app and nothing else. No itinerary, no hints, just a countdown and a little note every so often that makes them more curious, not less. "It's not a dinner. It's not a party. Stop guessing."

The reveal

The wrapping paper tears itself away

Then comes the moment the whole thing was building toward: the wrapping paper tears itself away on screen and the real surprise appears. Real paper-tear animation, real reaction, no spoilers beforehand. Whatever the surprise is, this is the first time they are seeing it.

On the road

Capsules that open along the way

Sealed capsules unlock as the trip unfolds, each one holding a note, a photo or a riddle to read out loud. Some open when a certain time arrives. Some wait until the car pulls up somewhere specific. Some open only when it is time to tap one by hand. A notification lands the second it happens, even if the app is closed and the phone has been locked in a pocket for an hour.

Every opened capsule gets crumpled up and tossed into a little pile in the corner, because the point was never to keep them pristine. It is to open them.

Then a trip companion

A plan that listens

From there the app quietly becomes something else: a day-by-day plan they can see, places to react to with a tap, and a way to suggest a change without anyone pulling out a group chat. If they would rather sleep in than hit a 9am stop, they say so, and the plan updates. No argument required.

Run from a phone

One control panel, live

The whole thing is run from a single control panel, from a phone. Release a capsule early. Send a message out of nowhere. Watch the plan change in real time as suggestions come in.

The person getting the surprise never sees any of it. They just feel like the trip is somehow always one step ahead of them, which, for those few days, it actually is.

  • Countdown and teaser notes
  • Capsules that open by time, place or tap
  • Instant notifications, even with the app closed
  • Paper-tear reveal
  • Live trip plan with suggestions
  • Phone control panel

This is not a template. Every custom order is built from scratch around one specific trip, one specific person, and whatever is going to make them feel like someone thought about this for weeks. Because someone did.
