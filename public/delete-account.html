Delete your account

Last updated October 2, 2026

You can delete your account and its data from any Brightmade Studios app at any time.

In the app

Open the app, go to Settings, then tap Delete account and confirm. Your account and data are removed right away from the app and within 30 days from our backups.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email contact@brightmadestudios.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my account and the app's name. We'll confirm the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in information
  • Everything you created or stored in the app
  • Any settings or preferences tied to your account

What we may keep

We may keep a record that the request was completed, and anything the law requires us to keep. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google, not by us.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings so you aren't charged again.
