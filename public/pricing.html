Photos and privacy

Photos, AI and privacy, in plain words

  • Guests never need an account, an email address or a phone number to join in.
  • If your screen takes photos, they are taken on our equipment. Each one gets its own private link, and only its QR code opens it.
  • Some screens use AI, for example to turn a photo into a portrait or to read a handwritten note. When one does, the photo is sent to an AI service from Google (Gemini) to do that, and we tell you which screens work this way before you book.
  • After your event we send the host a copy of the photos and delete them from our system. Any guest can skip a screen or ask us to delete their photo.

Questions about photos or privacy? Write to us before the day and we will answer plainly.

Questions we hear a lot

What can you put on a screen?

Almost anything you can describe: a game, a photo moment, a message wall, a countdown, a themed world. See the screen ideas for a taste, then tell us yours.

Does a different idea cost more?

No. A screen is $100 an hour whatever the theme. Company events are $150 an hour per screen, plus a one time fee for logo and theme work.

What do you need from us?

A little floor space and a power outlet for each screen, and a good internet connection. We check the details with you before the day.

Is AI really involved?

Some screens use it, and we like to say so. The privacy note above explains what is sent and what happens to it, and we tell you before you book which screens use AI.

What if the event runs long?

No problem. Extra time is billed in half hour steps at the same hourly price.

How do we book?

Send us your idea, the date, the place and roughly how many guests through the contact page. We write back with a clear quote.

Got an idea for a screen?

Tell us what you want to show, the date and roughly how many guests. We will write back with a clear quote.
