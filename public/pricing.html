Photos and privacy

Photos and privacy, in plain words

  • Kids never need an account, an email address or a phone number to join in.
  • Photos are taken on our equipment. Each one gets its own private link, and only its QR code opens it.
  • The Elfie Selfie Station and Letter to Santa use AI. For those, the photo is sent to an AI service from Google (Gemini) so it can make the elf portrait or read the letter. The Lion Mirror follows movement right on the device and does not send photos to AI.
  • After your event we send the host a copy of the photos and delete them from our system. Any family can skip a station or ask us to delete their photo.

Questions about photos or privacy? Write to us before the day and we will answer plainly.

Questions we hear a lot

Can we book just one station?

Yes. Each station is one screen at $100 an hour. Two screens are $200 an hour, and all three are $250 an hour as the Full Experience.

What do you need from us?

A little floor space and a power outlet for each station, and a good internet connection. We check the details with you before the day.

Can you match our theme?

Our holiday theme is ready now. If you would like a different look, or your company logo on the frame, tell us and we will quote it.

Is AI really involved?

Yes, and we like to say so. The elf portraits and the letter reading use AI. The photo privacy note above explains exactly what is sent and what happens to it.

What if the party runs long?

No problem. Extra time is billed in half hour steps at the same hourly price.
