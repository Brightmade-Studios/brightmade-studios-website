Photos and privacy

Photos, AI and privacy, in plain words

  • Guests never need an account, an email address or a phone number to join in.
  • If a station takes photos, they are taken on our equipment. Each one gets its own private link, and only its QR code opens it.
  • Some stations use AI: Elfie Selfie turns a photo into a portrait and Letter to Santa reads a handwritten wish list (both use Google Gemini), and Drawing Comes to Life sends the drawing to a 3D model service (Tripo). The station list says which ones, and we tell you again before you book.
  • After your event we send the host a copy of the photos and delete them from our system. Any guest can skip a station or ask us to delete their photo.

Questions about photos or privacy? Write to us before the day and we will answer plainly.

Questions we hear a lot

Which stations can I book?

Character Mirror, Photobooth, Letter to Santa, Drawing Comes to Life, Elfie Selfie, Snow Globe, Guest Book and the 360 Booth. See the stations for what each one does.

Does a different theme cost more?

No. A station is $100 an hour whatever the theme. Company events are quoted as a corporate package.

How does the 360 Booth price work?

On its own it is $200 an hour. Booked with any other station it is $150 an hour, and the other station stays $100 an hour.

What do you need from us?

A little floor space and a power outlet for each station, and a good internet connection. We check the details with you before the day.

Is AI really involved?

Some stations use it, and we like to say so. The privacy note above explains what is sent and what happens to it, and we tell you before you book which stations use AI.

What if the event runs long?

No problem. Extra time is billed in half hour steps at the same hourly price.
