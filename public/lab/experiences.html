What a screen can do

Whatever the theme, a screen usually does one or more of these. Combine them however you like.

Play

Games and moves that react to guests: wave, jump, dance, tap.

Capture

Photo moments with your own frame, and a QR code so guests keep them.

Show

Slideshows, countdowns, schedules, scoreboards, menus and shout-outs.

Tell

Message walls, guest books, wish lists and stories told on a big screen.

Surprise

Portraits, animations and little moments that make people say wow.

What we bring

  • The screen, camera and everything that plugs in.
  • A look built around your theme and event name.
  • Setup, running it all event long, and teardown.
  • A scan-to-save QR code for anything guests make.

What we need from you

  • A bit of floor space and a power outlet for each screen.
  • A good internet connection. We check this with you before the day.
  • A little time before doors open so we can set up.

Photos, AI and privacy, in plain words

  • Guests never need an account, an email address or a phone number to join in.
  • If your screen takes photos, they are taken on our equipment. Each one gets its own private link, and only its QR code opens it.
  • Some screens use AI, for example to turn a photo into a portrait or to read a handwritten note. When one does, the photo is sent to an AI service from Google (Gemini) to do that, and we tell you which screens work this way before you book.
  • After your event we send the host a copy of the photos and delete them from our system. Any guest can skip a screen or ask us to delete their photo.

Questions about photos or privacy? Write to us before the day and we will answer plainly.
