Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Brightmade Studios LLC ("Brightmade," "we," "us") builds apps, websites and customer portals. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, our customer portal and our apps, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • This website has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Our customer portal has logins for the customers we are building for.
  • Each app collects only what it needs to work, and each one is listed below.
  • You can ask us to see or delete your information at any time.

This website

This site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. Like any web host, Google receives basic technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it briefly in server logs under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't use this information to identify you.

The site does not set cookies or use analytics, and the fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

When you email us

If you write to contact@brightmadestudios.com, we keep your email address and message so we can reply and follow up. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Our project request form

If you use the "Start your custom app" form on this website, we collect the name, email address and message you type, and your budget or timeline if you choose to give it. The form sends them to us by email through Resend, our email provider, and the message reaches our business inbox. This website does not keep a copy. We use what you send only to reply and to talk about your project.

The Get Questly and referral form

If you use the Get Questly form on our Questly page, including through a referral link or code, we collect your name, email address and phone number, whether someone referred you and their name if you give it, and any note you add. It reaches our business inbox by email through Resend, the same way as the project request form, and this website does not keep a copy. We use it only to contact you about Questly and to apply a referral discount. If you do not buy, we delete your details within one year.

Our customer portal

When we are building something for you, we give you a login to our customer portal at customer.brightmadestudios.com. The portal is separate from this website and has these details:

  • What we keep: your email address and the first name you give us (we use it to greet you in emails), your project's name and optional photo, the steps and timeline of the project, the buttons you press (such as approve, confirm or request changes) with any notes you write, and any ideas you submit.
  • Passwords: you choose your own password from a link we email you. Google Firebase Authentication handles it, and we cannot see it. The portal also keeps a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Who can see it: only the people on your project (up to two logins) and Brightmade. Other customers cannot see your project.
  • Approvals are a record: your button presses and notes are kept as a permanent log that customers cannot edit or delete, so both sides can see what was agreed.
  • Emails: we email you a set-password link, a welcome message and, if your project has them switched on, short update notices. They are sent through Resend from noreply@brightmadestudios.com.
  • Providers: Google (Firebase) for sign-in and the database, Cloudflare for the service that sends the emails, and Resend for delivery.

We do not sell portal information or use it for advertising. If you ask us to delete your information, we will remove or anonymize your personal details, and we may keep the approval log so the project history stays accurate.

Our apps

Each app has its own section here that lists exactly what it collects, why, and which service providers process it. The same details appear in the app's privacy label on the App Store and its Data safety section on Google Play.

  • Questly, our chores and rewards app for families, has its own policy because children use it: the Questly Privacy Policy, which is also our children's privacy notice for Questly.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run our website and apps, such as Google (Firebase) for hosting and app data. They process information only on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments. Each app's section names the providers it uses.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires. When you delete your account, we delete its data as described on our account deletion page.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email contact@brightmadestudios.com from the address linked to your account, and we'll respond within 30 days, or tell you if we need more time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children through it. Questly is used by children, with a parent's consent, and how it handles children's information is set out in the Questly Privacy Policy. Our other apps are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and to collect as little as possible.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let app users know in the app.
